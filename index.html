Straight answers

Do you hold my money?

No. Your money stays at your broker. Cipha uses the account. We never take custody.

Do I need to know how to trade?

No. That’s the point. You connect the account. You don’t sit on charts, and you don’t click buy/sell here.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Trading can lose money. Cipha can wait, and you can stop it. It is not a payout guarantee.

How do I stop?

Disconnect or kill the desk. The agent loses the keys. Your broker account stays yours.

Which brokers?

Any broker or prop firm that gives you MetaTrader 5.

Why isn’t this WhatsApp signals?

Signals ask you to click. Cipha does not. The agent trades on your login. You watch the English log.
